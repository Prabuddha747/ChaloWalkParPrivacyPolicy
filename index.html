Chalo Walk Par ("Chalo Walk Par", "we", "us", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

By using Chalo Walk Par, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that is reasonably necessary to provide, maintain, secure and improve the functionality of Chalo Walk Par.

2.1 Account Information

Chalo Walk Par currently provides account creation through Google Sign-In.

When you use Google Sign-In, we may receive:

  • The name associated with your Google account.
  • The email address associated with your Google account.

We use this information to authenticate your account, maintain your profile and provide account-related functionality.

2.2 Profile Information

You may voluntarily provide information such as:

  • Display name
  • Age
  • Sex
  • Height
  • Weight
  • Daily step goal

This information may be used to provide fitness-related calculations, application insights and other functionality within Chalo Walk Par.

2.3 Activity and Fitness Information

Depending on the features you use, the App may process:

  • Step count
  • Walking distance
  • Walking duration
  • Estimated calories burned
  • Walking history
  • Fitness progress
  • Leaderboard-related activity
  • Group activity

2.4 Location Information

If you grant location permission and start an active GPS walk, Chalo Walk Par may collect precise location information to:

  • Display your position on the walking map.
  • Record your walking route.
  • Calculate walking distance.
  • Display your completed walking route.
  • Maintain your walk-session history.

Precise GPS location is not collected by Chalo Walk Par solely for advertising purposes.

2.5 Health Connect Information

If you grant permission through Android Health Connect, Chalo Walk Par may read permitted health and fitness information, such as step count and heart rate, for functionality within the App.

Health and fitness information obtained through Android health permissions is not used by us to personalize or target advertisements.

You can revoke Health Connect permissions at any time through Android Settings → Health Connect.

2.6 Device and Technical Information

Certain technical information may be processed when you use the App, including:

  • Device model and manufacturer
  • Android operating system version
  • Application version
  • Language and regional settings
  • IP address
  • Firebase identifiers
  • Push notification token
  • Advertising identifiers, where applicable
  • Crash information and diagnostic information

7. Location and GPS Walking

Chalo Walk Par uses precise location only when necessary for the GPS walking functionality and when the user has granted the required Android permission.

During an active GPS walk, location information may be processed to create a route and calculate walking statistics.

Chalo Walk Par does not intentionally use precise GPS walking routes for advertising personalization.

You may disable location access at any time through Android Settings. Disabling location may prevent the GPS walking functionality from operating correctly.

8. Step Tracking and Background Processing

Chalo Walk Par uses Android activity-recognition and step-counter capabilities to provide step tracking.

The application may use an Android foreground service to continue step tracking while the device screen is off or while the application is not actively visible.

Background step tracking is used to provide the App's core fitness functionality and is not used solely for advertising.

9. Health Connect

Chalo Walk Par may request access to Android Health Connect information where required for supported fitness features.

Health Connect access is permission-based. The App can only access data for which you have granted permission.

Health information obtained through Health Connect is used for fitness-related functionality within Chalo Walk Par.

Health Connect information is not used by Chalo Walk Par for personalized advertising or interest-based advertising.

You can revoke Health Connect permissions through:

Android Settings → Health Connect
